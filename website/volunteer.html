Volunteer as a State Ally

We need people in every state who can check facts against primary sources: confirming an old law is still in the current code, checking the reporting behind a contested death, or delivering a repeal request to their own city council. Nothing gets published or sent until a local ally has verified it.

Roles

Law verifier

Check reported segregation or sundown provisions against your state's or city's current official code, then mark them verified.

Case verifier

Review the sources behind contested-death reports before they are added to the public list.

CDC data exporter

Run two short CDC WONDER exports by hand. Step-by-step instructions.

Local deliverer

Deliver verified repeal requests to your council, commission or state legislators, and post updates.

Legal reviewer

Attorneys and law students: review draft repeal ordinances and bills before delivery.

Researcher

Search municipal code libraries, session laws and archives for provisions that were never repealed.

What happens after you sign up

  1. An editor welcomes you on your sign-up issue and adds the volunteer-active label.
  2. You get assigned to reports in your state, such as old-law reports from the tracker, contested-death reports, or data exports.
  3. You record what you checked and where, citing the official code section or the source URL, in a comment on the report.
Privacy: sign-ups are public GitHub issues, so share only what you're comfortable having public. Editors contact you by @mentioning you on GitHub.
