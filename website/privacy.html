Privacy Policy

Last updated November 9, 2026

The Federal CAREN Act project is a volunteer advocacy campaign. This page explains what we collect and how to have it deleted.

What we collect

  • Volunteer sign-ups: the name, email, optional phone number, state, area, roles, background, availability and experience you enter. These are stored in a private database and only campaign editors can see them. We publish only the number of volunteers per state.
  • Sign-in with Google or Facebook: your account ID with that provider, your name, your email address and your profile picture. We use these only to sign you in and fill in your sign-up form. We never post anything to your accounts.
  • Representative lookup: the address you enter goes from your browser straight to the U.S. Census Bureau geocoder to find your district. It is never sent to us or stored.
  • Learn progress: which modules you finished, quizzes you passed and quests you marked done, with the XP for each and the state you chose for team scores. Without signing in, this is linked only to a random ID created in your browser, not to your name. If you sign in, it is linked to your account so it follows you between devices. Team scores show only totals per state.
  • Comments and reports posted through GitHub are public on GitHub and covered by GitHub's own privacy policy.

We don't sell or share personal information, use advertising trackers, or use data for anything other than this campaign.

Deleting your data

To have your volunteer record or sign-in account deleted, open a deletion request. Include your name and the state you signed up in, but not your email or phone (the request is public). An editor will match the record and confirm by email. We delete records within 30 days. To clear your Learn progress on a device, use “Reset progress on this device” on the Learn page; deleting your account also deletes the progress linked to it. If you signed in with Facebook, removing this app from your Facebook settings (Settings & privacy → Apps and websites) also stops any further data sharing.
