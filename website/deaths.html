Contested Hanging Deaths and Unnatural Deaths by Race

Black Americans are still being found hanged, sometimes from trees. When those deaths are ruled suicides, families sometimes say the investigations were incomplete. This page tracks the disputed cases and sets them beside national death data, which shows Black Americans have the highest rate of hanging deaths ruled homicide.

How to read this page. A case appears here because the official finding is publicly disputed, not because a crime has been proven. Every case cites the reporting it comes from. Lynching has been a federal hate crime since the Emmett Till Antilynching Act (2022), and families can ask the FBI and the DOJ Civil Rights Division to review a death.

What other investigations have found

    National data: hanging and other unnatural deaths by race

    The CDC doesn't publish individual death records. It does publish yearly totals by race and by how a death was classified: suicide, homicide, accident, legal intervention, or undetermined. “Suffocation” is the CDC category that includes hanging and strangulation.

    Gaps we are working to close

    • No national case registry. No public database lists individual deaths ruled suicide that families dispute. This page's case list, together with partners such as JULIAN and the NAACP Legal Defense Fund (which has asked Congress for a commission on suspicious Black deaths), is a start.
    • State numbers by race have to be exported by hand from CDC WONDER. Volunteers: here's how.
    • Deaths in custody. Under the Death in Custody Reporting Act, agencies must report deaths in jails and prisons, but the Justice Department's published data is incomplete.

    Volunteer guide: exporting CDC WONDER data

    CDC only allows state-level data by race to be downloaded by hand through its website. Each export takes about 10 minutes, and there are two. CDC's data use restrictions apply: never try to identify individuals, and never work out or publish counts that WONDER marks “Suppressed.”

    Export 1: hanging deaths by state, race and official classification

    1. Open Underlying Cause of Death, Single Race and click I Agree on the data use restrictions.
    2. Section 1 (Organize table layout): Group results by State, then by Single Race 6, then by UCD - ICD Sub-Chapter. Leave the other two boxes as None.
    3. Section 2 (Location): leave as The United States, which means all states.
    4. Section 4 (Year): leave All Years. Combining years keeps fewer numbers suppressed.
    5. Section 6 (Cause of death): choose ICD-10 Codes. Use Search to find and add these three codes, then click Add after each:
      • X70: intentional self-harm by hanging, strangulation and suffocation
      • X91: assault by hanging, strangulation and suffocation
      • Y20: hanging, strangulation and suffocation, undetermined intent
    6. Section 7 (Other options): check Export Results and Show Suppressed Values. Click Send. A .txt file downloads.

    Export 2: all unnatural (injury) deaths by state, race and intent

    1. Start a new request on the same page. Section 1: group by State, Single Race 6, then UCD - Injury Intent.
    2. Section 6: choose Injury Intent and Mechanism. Under Intent, select Unintentional, Suicide, Homicide, Undetermined and Legal Intervention / Operations of War (Ctrl/Cmd-click to select several). Leave Mechanism as All Causes of Death.
    3. Section 7: check Export Results and Show Suppressed Values, then click Send.
