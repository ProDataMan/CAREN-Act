Narration voices

Every voice below reads the same passage. All are free to use: public domain, CC0, or CC BY (credit given in the README). Pick the ones you like and tell us their codes (for example libritts-96); the lessons will be narrated in them.

Computer voices are made from recordings of real readers, and the voice libraries don't record readers' backgrounds, so the most authentic option is a real narrator. A volunteer who records the lessons can replace any of these voices.
