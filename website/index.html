About the Map & Data Layers

All layers remain available and toggleable. High-priority locations (overlaps of high lynching counts, residual discriminatory language, elevated police violence or disproportionate minority contact, and larger minority populations) are highlighted with larger or bolder markers, but no data is filtered out. Sample markers are illustrative and linked to real public sources. Full national datasets are large; production versions can load GeoJSON.

Prioritization (without filtering): Areas with multiple overlapping factors (high historical lynchings + residual ordinances + higher rates of police killings or disproportionate arrests/contacts with minorities + larger minority populations) are emphasized for advocacy focus. This does not remove or hide other locations.
  • Priority heat map: blends the sample locations from every layer, weighted by how many advocacy factors overlap there (lynching history, police violence, residual ordinances, minority population share, hate crimes). Warmer areas are where more factors overlap. The weights are illustrative, not a statistical index.
  • Hate Crimes: FBI UCR. Crime Data Explorer.
  • Lynchings: EJI >4,000 racial terror lynchings (1877–1950). EJI map.
  • Police Killings & Disproportionate Contact: Mapping Police Violence tracks fatal police encounters. Black Americans are disproportionately represented relative to population. mappingpoliceviolence.org. Disproportionate arrests/contacts drawn from public analyses and BJS patterns.
  • Minority Populations: Census-based patterns of higher Black, Latino, Native, and other minority shares (illustrative urban and southern examples).
  • Sundown / Redlining: Historical (Loewen database, HOLC maps via Mapping Inequality) shown in one color. Residual/zombie ordinances (obsolete text still in some codes until repealed; unenforceable) shown in a different highlight color. Example residual case cleaned up: Boynton Beach, FL (2024).
  • Allies: Color-coded by position and potential influence (e.g., Judiciary chairs/ranking members darker, other members lighter, local officials different hue). Sample only; expand with public records.
