Making discriminatory 911 calls illegal nationwide. Based on San Francisco's CAREN Act (Police Code §637).

About the Map

Layers are illustrative based on public data sources and are not exhaustive or real-time. Use for awareness and advocacy. See README for data notes and limitations.

  • Hate Crimes: Aggregated from FBI UCR reports. Full data at FBI Crime Data Explorer.
  • Lynchings: Based on Equal Justice Initiative research. See EJI map.
  • Historical: Southern states with documented histories of slavery and Jim Crow laws (most formal laws repealed or struck down).
  • Sundown / Redlining: Histories of exclusionary practices (James Loewen; HOLC maps via Mapping Inequality). Formal sundown ordinances are not currently in force.
  • Allies: Sample of members of Congress with civil rights records. Contact your representatives.
