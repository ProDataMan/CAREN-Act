Contact Your Representatives

Enter your address to find your two U.S. Senators and your U.S. Representative. We draft a personal letter to each one and give you their official contact form, phone number, and office address. Your address is sent only to the U.S. Census Bureau geocoder to find your district. It is never stored.

Member data: @unitedstates/congress-legislators (public domain, updated as membership changes). District lookup: U.S. Census Geocoder (119th Congress boundaries) or Census ZIP-to-district relationship files. Members of Congress don't publish email addresses, so use the official contact form; it is the channel offices track. Members shown serve in the 119th Congress through January 3, 2027. Several states redrew districts for the 120th Congress.
