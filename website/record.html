Lend your voice

Every lesson is narrated by a computer voice. A real person's voice, especially from the communities these lessons are about, means more. Record a lesson here; we'll line your recording up with the text so the words highlight as you speak, and credit you by name.

How it works:
  1. Pick a lesson and read the script below aloud while recording. Mistakes are fine: pause, and re-read the sentence.
  2. Download your recording and upload it to Google Drive, Dropbox or iCloud with a share link (or record in any app you like).
  3. Send us the link with the form at the bottom. An editor listens, aligns it, and publishes it, usually within a week.
Tips: a quiet room with soft furnishings (a closet full of clothes works well), the phone or mic about a hand's width from your mouth, a glass of water, and a natural, unhurried pace. Read the text as written so the highlighting lines up.
