How we got here, and the way out

Why are Black, Brown and Native people still harassed, policed, deported and pushed to the margins, even while they meet or beat every standard set for them? These short lessons trace it back to where it started, using laws, court records and data, and every one ends with something you can do about it.

There is no forgotten ancient secret. The record shows something more useful: race was built, by people, through laws we can name, for reasons we can name. What was built can be taken apart. Start with module 1 →

How to read these lessons

Every claim carries one of three labels, so you always know how solid the ground is. Lawmakers, reporters and opponents check sources, and one weak claim can discredit a hundred strong ones.

Modules

Each one is a 5–10 minute read with a timeline, primary sources, map links, a short quiz and a quest. Want to teach one? Every written module has a free teach-in kit with slides, a handout and a facilitator guide.

Source detective

Ten claims you'll hear about race and history. Is each one documented, debated, or a belief or myth? Get 8 right to earn the quest.

Quest board

Learning counts; acting counts more. Bigger tasks earn more XP. The quests run from the campaign itself to showing up for your neighbors, giving your professional skills, and (for anyone who has done well) helping the community you came from build ownership, not just receive donations. Quests are on the honor system for now: when you've done one, mark it.
