# San Francisco CAREN Act Documents

- `SF-CAREN-Act.pdf`: Ordinance No. 219-20 (File No. 200735), adding Police Code §637. Downloaded from the San Francisco Board of Supervisors: https://sfbos.org/sites/default/files/o0219-20.pdf
- Public link: https://prodataman.github.io/CAREN-Act/docs/SF-CAREN-Act.pdf

Legislative history (from the PDF's last page): committee amendment of the whole on Sept 24, 2020; recommended Oct 8, 2020; passed on first reading 11–0 on Oct 20, 2020; finally passed 11–0 on Oct 27, 2020.

Key provisions:
- Unlawful to knowingly cause a peace officer to arrive at a location to contact a person with the specific intent, based on a listed protected characteristic, to infringe constitutional rights, discriminate, harass/humiliate/embarrass, expel from a lawful place, or damage reputation or financial interests.
- Private civil cause of action with damages of at least $1,000 plus attorneys' fees and costs, and possible punitive damages.
- The findings (Sec. 1(c)) state it is not intended to discourage calls about real danger or actual crime. There is no separate statutory good-faith defense in the SF text.
